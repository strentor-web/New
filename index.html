FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a gym?

No. Programs are designed around the equipment you have — from resistance bands and dumbbells at home to a full gym.

I've never trained before. Is this for me?

Yes. Beginners get a safe, progressive start; experienced lifters get programming to push further.

How does online coaching work?

You get your plan digitally, train on your schedule, share videos for form feedback, and meet your coach for regular check-ins.

Where do you coach?

We focus on India, the UAE and Singapore, and coach clients anywhere online.

Is this only for wheelchair users?

STRENTOR is built for wheelchair users and people who train seated. If you have another adaptive need, get in touch and we'll tell you honestly whether we're a fit.
